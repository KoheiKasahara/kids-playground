# Working vehicle illustrations: easy level

Created 2026-10-03 using the built-in image_gen tool, one independent generation per asset. Original educational illustrations based on recognizable Japanese vehicle forms, not photographs or manufacturer artwork. The user's supplied vehicle chart informed the overall child-friendly encyclopedia direction. Manufacturer names identify form references only; images omit lettering and brand logos.

All final files are in `public/images/working-vehicles/`, RGB WebP, 768 × 512, quality 86. Generated PNGs were resized and converted with Pillow without content edits. Visual inspection confirmed full vehicles, recognizable equipment, left-facing composition, white background, no people and no labels.

### ambulance.webp

```text
Use case: illustration-story. Asset: Japanese children's working vehicle quiz. Create one original children's encyclopedia illustration of a modern Japanese Toyota HiMedic / HiAce high-roof ambulance: white tall van body, broad RED emergency roof beacon, red horizontal stripe on body, short sloped front nose, two axles, sliding side door, rear patient compartment windows. Recognizably Japanese ambulance, not an American truck. Clean hand-painted gouache flat shapes, very subtle paper texture and soft shading, slightly chubby/deformed but mechanically recognizable real Japanese working vehicle. Friendly rounded forms, crisp silhouette, dark gray tires, muted blue-gray windows. Pure white background, no scene, very faint contact shadow only. Center the entire vehicle in landscape 3:2 composition, front facing LEFT, three-quarter front-left view with the side clearly visible. Generous 10 percent white margins, vehicle large enough for mobile quiz. No people, faces, eyes, logos, lettering, numbers, captions, watermarks, border, other vehicles. Do not place brand emblems or readable license plates.
```
