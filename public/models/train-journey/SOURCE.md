# Kenney Train Kit 1.1

- Official source: https://kenney.nl/assets/train-kit
- Archive: https://kenney.nl/media/pages/assets/train-kit/cf8521d625-1727040883/kenney_train-kit.zip
- Retrieved: 2026-09-10
- Author: Kenney. Additional credits: Guus Vermeulen, Tony Schaer.
- License: CC0 1.0. The original `License.txt` is included in this directory.

Eight original GLB files (908,620 bytes total), the shared 512×512 palette
`Textures/colormap.png`, and three original preview PNGs are used. Models are
unmodified. At runtime the rear bullet cab is rotated 180 degrees, the steam
wheels are animated, and `spline-segment.glb` is instanced as sleepers along
the continuous generated railway. No external asset CDN is used at runtime.

The island, rail curves, station, bridge, tunnel, scenery, UI, and procedural
audio were created for kids-playground. The PWA's existing GLB/PNG precache
rules include these assets.
